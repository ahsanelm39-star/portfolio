Gulf Horizon Cargo & Marine Freight

How I architected an enterprise bilingual logistics platform with an interactive Request For Quote (RFQ) system for a Kuwait and Saudi Arabian freight operator on behalf of a B2B marketing agency.

CLIENT MARKET Kuwait & Saudi Arabia
DELIVERY ROLE Fulfillment Web Partner
PLATFORM Responsive Web + GHL CRM
LANGUAGES Arabic (RTL) & English
GULF HORIZON • CORPORATE B2B PORTAL 100% WHITE-LABEL

[01] The Agency Dilemma

A B2B marketing agency secured a major retainer with a cross-border cold-chain and marine freight provider operating between Shuwaikh Port (Kuwait) and Dammam / Riyadh (Saudi Arabia). The client's existing website was a dense, 10-year-old portal loaded with technical PDFs that made quotation inquiries nearly impossible on mobile devices.

The agency needed to present a modern corporate aesthetic that would establish instant institutional credibility with enterprise procurement managers, while keeping the implementation turnaround predictable and completely under the agency's brand.

[02] Information Architecture & RFQ Funnel

To transform technical logistics data into actionable lead flows:

  • 3-Step RFQ Calculator: Simplified the complex rate estimation process into Origin, Destination, and Cargo Type (Reefer, Dry, Hazmat), funneling high-value inquiries straight to the operations team.
  • Fleet & Cold-Chain Specifications: Structured clear visual capability cards for -25°C pharmaceutical-certified transport and 24/7 GCC border clearance procedures.
  • Bilingual Enterprise Parity: Mapped complete Arabic technical maritime terms alongside English commercial trade vernacular without broken RTL flexbox containers.

[03] Results & Hand-Off

The agency received the staging website in 6 business days. The agency's sales team used the new site during their executive presentation, impressing the logistics board and securing a secondary retainer for regional search campaigns.
