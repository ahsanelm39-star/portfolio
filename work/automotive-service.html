AUTOMOTIVE & LUXURY CARE • WHITE-LABEL FULFILLMENT

Apex Performance & Auto Detailing

How I transformed a 2-page brief and raw WhatsApp notes into a high-end bilingual booking website inside GoHighLevel for a Kuwait marketing agency — delivered in 5 business days.

CLIENT MARKET Kuwait (Shuwaikh Hub)
DELIVERY ROLE White-Label Web Builder
PLATFORM GoHighLevel + Custom CSS
LANGUAGES Bilingual (Arabic & English)
APEX PERFORMANCE • LIVE DELIVERED STAGING AGENCY READY

[01] The Agency Dilemma

A boutique digital marketing company in Kuwait closed an exclusive retainer with a premier automotive customization and ceramic coating workshop in Shuwaikh Industrial Area. The client had high aesthetic standards: dark luxury aesthetics, distinct service tiers for supercars versus daily vehicles, and an insistence on a seamless Arabic RTL and English experience.

However, the agency's internal designers were already stretched thin managing ongoing Meta, TikTok, and Google Ads creative production for six other clients. The account manager needed someone who could take a rough brief, asset folder, and service price sheet, and independently engineer the complete website without constant check-ins.

[02] My Fulfillment Approach

Rather than treating it as a generic web design task, I structured the build around the Gulf automotive consumer's buying psychology:

  • Vehicle-Specific Pathways: Clear segregation for Exotics / Supercars, Luxury SUVs, and Daily Commuters, allowing customers to self-identify their service scope within 5 seconds.
  • WhatsApp Quotation Architecture: Instead of friction-heavy 10-field web forms, I built an interactive package selector that dynamically generates a pre-filled WhatsApp message with selected vehicle model and package choice.
  • Arabic RTL Layout Mastery: Styled with IBM Plex Sans Arabic to ensure typography was crisp and authoritative rather than feeling like an afterthought translation.

[03] GoHighLevel & Custom CSS Execution

The agency hosts client funnels inside GoHighLevel. Standard GHL templates often look rigid and unpolished. To elevate the client's brand into a true luxury tier, I implemented custom CSS layers:

// Highlights of CSS injection delivered inside GHL sub-account:
• Glassmorphism card backdrops with backdrop-filter: blur(14px)
• Custom directional overrides for [dir="rtl"] Arabic views
• Sticky bottom mobile booking bar with 1-tap WhatsApp consultation
• Performance optimizations ensuring mobile load speed under 1.4s

[04] Delivery & Hand-Off

The staging environment was delivered to the agency's account director on day 4 of the sprint. After one minor round of text adjustments on package warranties, the agency presented the website to the workshop owner.

Key Fulfillment Benefit: The agency's internal ad team remained 100% focused on campaign launch, while the website was delivered on time under the agency's brand without a single direct client interaction.
